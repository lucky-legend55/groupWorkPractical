Impacts of Computing
  • Home
  • Education
  • Healthcare
  • Environment

Benefits

  • Learning is often more engaging as teachers have wider access to resources to create their lessons
  • Students can find the most up-to-date information for any research projects
  • Teaching can still take place from home using remote learning software
  • Digital literacy and critical thinking skills being taught thorugh consistent use are essential skills for the future workforce
  • Keeps parents informed about their child's progress and other relevant information

Drawbacks

  • Children can quite easily go off task and get distracted
  • Heavy safeguarding needs to be in place to monitor children's activity
  • Not everyone will have access to the same quality of technology
